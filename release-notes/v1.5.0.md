Seasons, player search and loot quality.

- **By season** table: chests per season, from the game version of each replay (v42 = Chapter 7 Season 4, v32 = Chapter 2 Remix...).
- **By mode** table (Solo, Duos, Squads, Zero Build, Ranked...).
- **Who you play with**: your most frequent teammates, with the chests they open.
- **Find a player**: type a name and see every match where that player was recorded, with their chests; click a result to open the match.
- **Loot quality**: colored rarity dots on what came out of your chests, and a rarity summary per match.
- **`players.csv`**: one row per player per match, for spreadsheets. Names are quoted and formulas neutralized.
- Replays still on disk are re-analyzed automatically only if the analysis changed (it did not in this version).
